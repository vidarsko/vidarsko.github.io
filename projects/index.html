Projects

Research, teaching, and science communication — the different threads of my work.

Research

Learning with AI

My current research programme: a forthcoming book, a peer-reviewed study of how physics professors use generative AI, a course for teachers, a blog, and a podcast.

Explore this project

ComFiT — a field-theory simulation library

During my PhD I worked with field theories containing topological defects; my co-PhD student Jonas Rønning worked on related theories. After finishing, we built ComFiT together — an open-source Python library for simulating computational field theory with topological defects, with full documentation and test coverage, published in the Journal of Open Source Software.

Teaching teachers

Profag KI-kurs

A four-module professional-development programme on AI in teaching, built with Andreas Haraldsrud at KURT (UiO): generative-AI foundations and prompt design, teaching activities and differentiation, programming with AI, and assessment. Run for teacher groups across several Norwegian counties each semester since 2025.

"Balanced — it looks at both the advantages and disadvantages of AI, and doesn't sweep the challenges under the rug." — course participant, spring 2025

Co-supervisor — student projects

Currently co-supervising two students at the University of Oslo, working with computational field-theory methods built on the ComFiT framework.

Podcasts

Under Kappa

A bi-weekly science communication podcast, co-founded and co-hosted with Even Garvang, analyzing the scientific plausibility of superheroes — physics, biology, and everything in between. Close to 70 episodes since April 2023, national TV and radio coverage, and the Norwegian Physical Society's 2025 outreach prize.

Visit Under Kappa

REAL læring

A podcast made as senior lecturer at KURT (UiO) about the learning sciences in higher education — seasons on ChatGPT and learning, the different institutes at the MN faculty, and teaching formats such as compulsory assignments and group sessions. A new season on generative AI is in production.

KIosk

A podcast where I talk to teachers who have actually tried something with AI in their own classroom — what worked, what didn't, and what they'd do differently.

Talks & media

Recurring panelist on NRK's Abels tårn (P2) since 2022, repeat guest on Andreas Wahl's podcast Jøss?, and occasional science expert for TV2 — plus talks and teacher workshops on AI and physics across Norway and the Nordics. The full, dated list lives on the CV page.

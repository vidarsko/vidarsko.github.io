About me

I'm a physicist based in Stockholm, currently a senior lecturer at the University of Oslo's Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences. My PhD work was on phase-field modeling of crystal deformation and microplasticity — how grain boundaries move, and what that means for how a material yields.

My current research looks at how AI shapes teaching and learning more broadly.

Alongside research and teaching, I work in science communication: I co-host the podcast Under Kappa, appear regularly on NRK's Abels tårn, and give talks on AI and learning.
